With arca

One library.
Every screen.

arca keeps your movies, photos and music on a computer you run at home: your hub. Your laptop and phone sync with it, and arca TV puts it all on the biggest screen in the house.

Start a film on your laptop and finish it on the sofa. The photos from your phone are on the TV the same day. Your albums and playlists are where you left them. No subscriptions, no company server in the middle.

Meet arca ↗

Movies, photos, music and the photo screensaver need an arca hub, kept on while you play.

Already have arca? Connect your TV.
  1. Keep your hub and TV on the same network. In the hub’s Settings → Local network, enable Allow HTTP connections. TV playback uses this local connection.
  2. Link a phone album to a hub folder, or choose Set up as library… → Photos in an existing folder’s ⋯ menu. For music, choose Set up as library… → Audio. For movies and shows, choose Set up as library… → Movies & TV on a hub folder.
  3. On the hub, open Settings → Authorized devices → Manage devices and generate a device pairing code. On the TV, open Settings → Hub; it finds the hub and opens the code screen. Enter the six digits, or choose Enter address if discovery finds nothing.
  4. arca Movies, arca Photos and arca Music become available for the libraries your hub exposes, and Watch next appears on Home. Keep the hub on while the TV plays them.

Install

Make yourself at home.

For Google TV and Android TV running Android 9 or newer. You’ll need a computer on the same network and access to the TV’s debugging options. Setup varies by TV; LG webOS and Samsung Tizen are not supported.

In the examples below, replace <TV_IP> with your TV’s IP address before running commands.

  1. 1

    On the TV

    Settings → System → About, press Android TV OS build seven times. That opens Developer options, where you turn on USB debugging so your computer can install apps. Note the TV’s IP address under Network & Internet.

  2. 2

    On your computer

    Open an AI coding assistant and say:

    Install arca TV (github.com/satoshi-ltd/arca-tv) on my TV at <TV_IP>. I live in Tokyo.

    Replace <TV_IP> with your TV’s IP address and Tokyo with your city.

    Use an assistant with terminal access. It builds and installs the app, asking before installing missing tools. Accept the TV’s debugging prompt with Always allow. You can mention a domo server in the same request.

  3. 3

    Back on the TV

    Open arca TV → Home screen → arca TV. Accept the debugging prompt with Always allow. The welcome lets you connect an arca hub or start without one.

  1. 1

    On the TV

    Open Settings → System → About and press Android TV OS build seven times. In Developer options, turn on USB debugging, then note the TV’s IP address under Network & Internet. Menu names vary by TV.

  2. 2

    Tools

    A JDK (17 or newer), the Android SDK command-line tools and adb. On a Mac:

    brew install --cask temurin android-commandlinetools android-platform-tools
sdkmanager "build-tools;34.0.0" "platforms;android-34"
  3. 3

    Build

    git clone https://github.com/satoshi-ltd/arca-tv && cd arca-tv && ./build.sh

    The first run creates config.yaml. Optionally add your city or domo settings locally, then run ./build.sh again. The result is ArcaTV.apk.

  4. 4

    Install

    adb connect <TV_IP>:5555
adb devices
adb -s <TV_IP>:5555 install -r ArcaTV.apk

    When the TV asks Allow USB debugging?, tick Always allow and accept. Wait until the TV appears as device before installing.

    On TCL, also allow arca TV to start:

    adb -s <TV_IP>:5555 shell "appops set com.satoshilimited.tv AUTO_START allow || appops set com.satoshilimited.tv APP_AUTO_START allow"
  5. 5

    Back on the TV

    Open arca TV → Home screen → arca TV. Accept the debugging prompt with Always allow. The welcome lets you connect an arca hub or start without one.

Keep the project folder and arca-tv.keystore: updates need the same signing key. Paste service tokens into config.yaml yourself; never put them in an assistant chat.

Before you start

A few good
questions.

Do I need an arca hub?

Home, your apps and the weather work without one. Connect a hub with a movie, photo or music library to use arca Movies, Watch next, arca Photos, arca Music and your photos as a screensaver. The TV streams from the hub, so keep it on and reachable while you watch or listen.

What do I need to run arca?

A computer or server you control can be your hub; dedicated hardware is optional. arca keeps shared folders and their history there and syncs complete copies to your other devices. The TV is a player: it reads the library without changing your files.

See how to set up arca →

What changes on my TV?

arca TV becomes your home screen and screensaver and disables selected built-in apps. It does not uninstall them or require root. Choose arca TV → Home screen → Original to restore the recorded home screen and re-enable apps it disabled. After repeated crashes, a watchdog attempts the same recovery.

Does anything leave my home?

Your movies, photos and music stream directly from your hub over your network. Weather and outdoor air requests send your selected city’s coordinates to Open-Meteo; city searches send the name you enter. Like any online request, these expose your public IP to the service. arca TV adds no analytics. Android TV and the apps you open have their own network activity and privacy policies.

Will it play all my videos?

arca TV plays the files from your hub as they are, with their audio tracks and subtitles. When the TV cannot play a file’s audio, the hub offers a compatible audio track; the picture is never re-encoded, so a video format the TV cannot decode will not play.

Which TVs does it support?

arca TV targets Google TV and Android TV with Android 9 or newer. Installation needs debugging access; menus and manufacturer restrictions vary. It does not run on LG webOS or Samsung Tizen.
